# DORMBIT demo video: shot list

Planned at 150 words per minute with `node scripts/media/script-timing.mjs`: 348 words, 2:26.6 of speech, **2:39 total** (target 2:35-2:45, Requirement 14.2). The video project sets each segment's length from the recorded voice file, so the real times will shift a little. If the read is slower, the music-only beats get shorter first.

Sources: **native** is a window-only recording of the macOS app on the Dockline fixture (needs the user's Screen Recording approval). **web** is a Playwright take of the live demo at 1920×1080 (Requirement 15.2). **motion** is rendered in the video project. Third-party names appear as plain text only, and no logos are used (Requirement 15.9).
