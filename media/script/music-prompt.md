# DORMBIT demo video: music prompt

One instrumental track, at least 2:50 long. It sits under the voice, so it carries mood and rhythm, not melody. The times follow the shot list. Generators rarely hit them exactly, so I cut the track to the picture in the edit.

## Main prompt

Under 1,000 characters, so it fits most tools' prompt or style field:

```text
Instrumental score for a tech product demo, 2:50 long, 120 BPM, D minor. Cinematic analog synth electronica with a subtle mad-scientist lab feel: precise and confident, lightly eerie, never campy. Analog polysynth, modular blips, electric hum, wordless choir pad, a sparse theremin-like sine lead. 0:00 dormant: low drone, slow ticking clock, relay clicks, faint heartbeat kick. 0:14 the jolt: electric spark and a deep sub-bass hit. 0:14 to 1:03: steady pulse, filtered 16th-note arpeggio, tight kick and clap, warm sub bass, slow build. 1:03 to 1:34: add a pulsing pad and hi-hats. 1:34 to 1:52: drop to a tense low pulse, one beat of silence, then glitchy stutters. 1:52 to 2:14: modulate to D major, warm rising chords. 2:14 to 2:40: full groove, most energy. 2:40: final hit, clean ring-out to silence, no fade loop. Sparse midrange under narration, no busy lead. No vocals, no lyrics, no organ, no jump scares.
```

## Short prompt

For fields with a tight character limit:

```text
Instrumental cinematic analog synth, 120 BPM, D minor to D major, ticking clock, electric spark hit, 16th arpeggio, eerie choir pad, sparse midrange for narration, no vocals
```

## Section tags

For tools that read a structure field:

```
[Intro: dormant, drone, ticking clock, 14 s]
[Impact: electric spark, sub hit]
[Section A: 120 BPM pulse, filtered arpeggio, 48 s]
[Section B: add pad and hats, 31 s]
[Break: drop, one beat of silence, glitch stutters, 18 s]
[Section C: modulate to D major, warm lift, 22 s]
[Section D: full groove, 26 s]
[Outro: final hit, ring out to silence]
[Instrumental]
```
