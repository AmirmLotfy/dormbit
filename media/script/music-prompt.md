# DORMBIT demo video: music prompt

One instrumental track, at least 2:50 long. It sits under the voice, so it carries mood and rhythm, not melody. The sections below line up with the shot list. Most generators won't hit the times exactly; I cut and stretch to the picture in the edit.

## Full prompt

Use this where the tool accepts a long description:

> Instrumental score for a product demo, about 2:50, 120 BPM, starting in D minor. Cinematic analog-synth electronica with a mad-scientist laboratory feel: precise, confident, and lightly eerie, never campy.
>
> 0:00-0:14, dormant: a low drone, a slow ticking clock, faint mechanical relay clicks, a distant heartbeat-like kick, tape hiss. Sparse and suspenseful.
>
> 0:14, the jolt: a sharp electrical spark and a deep sub-bass hit, as if a machine switches on.
>
> 0:14-1:03, the lab: a steady 120 BPM pulse. A filtered 16th-note analog arpeggio, a tight electronic kick and clap, warm sub bass. It builds slowly.
>
> 1:03-1:34, the machine: add a pulsing pad and closed hi-hats, and open the filter a little. More momentum, same restraint.
>
> 1:34-1:52, the fault: everything drops to a tense low pulse. One beat of total silence, then glitchy bit-crushed stutters and a detuned stab. Ominous but controlled.
>
> 1:52-2:14, the resurrection: modulate to D major. Warm, rising chords. An emotional lift, not louder.
>
> 2:14-2:40, two bodies: the full groove returns with the arpeggio and the major chords. The most energetic part.
>
> 2:40-2:50, ending: one final hit and a clean ring-out to silence. No fade-out loop.
>
> Instruments: analog polysynth, modular blips, electric hum, a subtle wordless choir pad, and a sine-wave lead like a theremin, used sparingly. Mix for voice-over: keep the midrange sparse and put no busy lead under speech. No vocals, no spoken words, no lyrics, no church organ, no screams, no jump-scare stingers.

## Short prompt (for fields with a character limit)

> Instrumental cinematic analog synth, 120 BPM, D minor to D major, ticking clock, electric spark hit, 16th arpeggio, eerie choir pad, sparse midrange for narration, no vocals
